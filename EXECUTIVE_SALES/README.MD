🛒 Executive Sales Dashboard


 📊 Overview

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to provide an executive-level overview of e-commerce sales performance, including sales, profit, customers, orders, and customer ratings.
The dashboard transforms sales data into clear and actionable business insights using KPIs, charts, trends, and interactive filters.

🎯 Key Features

- 💰 Total Sales & Total Profit
- 📦 Total Orders & Customers
- ⭐ Average Customer Rating
- 🏷️ Brand-wise Profit Analysis
- 📊 Category & Sub-Category Sales Analysis
- 📈 Monthly Sales Trends
- 🌍 Region-wise Sales
- 🗺️ State-wise Sales Analysis
- 💵 Profit Analysis by Product Group
- 💳 Payment Method Analysis

🎛️ Interactive Filters

- Year
- Month
- Region
- Category
- Order Status
- Payment Method

🛠️ Tools Used

 -Tableau  – Dashboard & Data Visualization
 -Microsoft Excel – Data Cleaning & Preparation

💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

- Top-performing brands and categories
- Most profitable product groups
- Regional and state-wise sales performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Customer and order performance
- Popular payment methods

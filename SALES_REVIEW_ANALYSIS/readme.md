# 📊 Sales Review and Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Review and Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive sales analytics dashboard designed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **sales, profit, orders, quantity, discounts, regional performance, category performance, customer performance, and profitability**.

This project was created as part of my **Data Analytics Portfolio** using **Tableau**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify high-performing regions and categories
- Track sales trends over time
- Analyze profit performance across regions and sub-categories
- Identify top customers based on sales
- Compare regional profit ratios
- Understand the impact of discounts on sales performance
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau** – Data visualization and dashboard development
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and cleaning
- **Data Analysis** – Sales, profit, customer, regional and category analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

Some of the insights available from the dashboard include:

- **West** is the strongest region in terms of overall sales.
- **Technology** generates the highest sales among the major categories.
- **Copiers** show strong profitability compared with other sub-categories.
- **East** has a relatively high profit ratio compared with the other regions.
- **Leo Jackson** is the highest-ranked customer based on sales.
- Sales performance varies significantly across states and regions.
- The dashboard allows users to identify high-performing and low-performing areas quickly.
